MagiCut — Privacy Policy

App: MagiCut — Video Editor & Maker (com.magicut.app)
Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MagiCut ("we", "us"), operated by GumLabs, handles information when you use the MagiCut video editor app.

1. Your videos and media stay on your device

MagiCut edits your videos and photos on your device. Trimming, merging, filters, effects, text & stickers, music, speed, reverse/boomerang, voice, blur, and background changes are processed locally, and exports are saved to your device. We do not upload or store your personal media on our servers for editing.

The AI Magic Background feature runs entirely on your device using Google ML Kit Selfie Segmentation — your video frames are not sent to any server for background processing.

Some features use the network: the Music tool can fetch royalty-free tracks from the third-party Jamendo service; the video downloader downloads from a direct link you provide; and Cast to TV streams your video to a Chromecast on your local network. None of these send your personal videos to us.

2. Information we (and our providers) collect

  • Advertising identifiers & ad data — we use Google AdMob to show ads, which may collect an advertising ID, device identifiers, and coarse/derived location for ad delivery and measurement.
  • Purchase information — Google Play Billing processes MagiCut Pro subscriptions, lifetime purchases, and Remove Ads; we receive purchase status to unlock features. We do not receive your full payment details.
  • Diagnostics & analytics — we use Google Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash logs and Google Firebase Analytics to collect in-app usage events (e.g. which tools are used, exports) to improve the app. These are associated with a random app-instance identifier, not your name or email.

3. Permissions

  • Media / storage — to read videos and photos you choose to edit or play, and to save your exports.
  • Internet / network state — for ads, the music library, the downloader, and purchase verification.
  • Private Folder — a 4-digit PIN and any videos you move into it are stored in the app's private on-device storage; we cannot see them.

4. How we use information

To provide and improve the app, deliver and measure ads, process purchases and subscriptions, fix crashes, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Ads & consent

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. For users in the EEA/UK, we request consent (via Google's UMP) before serving personalized ads. You can reduce personalized ads in your device settings. MagiCut Pro or the Remove Ads purchase removes ads.

6. Subscriptions

MagiCut Pro is an auto-renewing subscription with an optional free trial; it renews unless cancelled before the period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in Google Play > Subscriptions. A one-time lifetime purchase is also offered.

7. Children

MagiCut is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention & deletion

Locally processed media remains on your device under your control.

Delete your data in the app: go to Settings > Delete my data. This erases the data MagiCut stores on your device — your video list, favourites, watch history, drafts, saved settings, and cached files — and resets the app's analytics identity so past diagnostics can no longer be linked to your install. Videos in the Private Folder and its PIN are deleted only if you choose to include them, since they may be your only copy. Your own media in shared storage (e.g. your gallery and exported videos) is never touched — those files belong to you and remain on your device.

Deleting data in the app clears what is stored on your device and unlinks future data collection. Diagnostics and analytics data already uploaded is retained and deleted according to Google Firebase's retention periods; the app cannot recall it once sent. Your MagiCut Pro entitlement is a Google Play purchase, not personal data, and is not affected — it can be restored after reinstalling.

For any other data held by our providers (ads, analytics, purchases), or any questions, you may also request access or deletion by contacting us at sinn061094@gmail.com.
